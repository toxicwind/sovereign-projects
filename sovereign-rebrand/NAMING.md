# Sovereign Naming Rationale

## Why the original names are good

- **pi** (2 chars): Mathematical constant. Fundamental, universal, core. Memorable because it's taught to every child.
- **zed** (3 chars): Last letter of alphabet. Ultimate, final, edgy. Short and punchy.
- **llama-swap** (hyphenated): Descriptive. Contains the animal (SEO) + the action (clarity). Immediately understood.

## Why our new names are better

| Original | New | Why |
|----------|-----|-----|
| pi | **tau** | τ = 2π. The mathematical community's proposed "better" circle constant. A full circle is τ radians, not 2π. We took π and made it whole. |
| zed | **qed** | QED = "quod erat demonstrandum" = end of proof. Z is the end of the alphabet. QED is the end of the argument. We took Zed and made it definitive. |
| llama-swap | **herd** | A group of llamas is a herd. Swapping is one action. Herding is orchestration. We took swap and made it scale. |

## Legal safety

- No trademarked terms in repo names (no "Pi", "Zed", "LLaMA")
- No trademarked terms in package names (npm/cargo/crate names should be tau/qed/herd)
- No co-branding (never "tau/pi", "qed-zed", "herd-llama")
- Factual statements only ("we started from" not "we own")
- License separation (upstream MIT, your SOL)
- Automated sync proves ongoing relationship, not abandonment
